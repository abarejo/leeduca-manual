The Leetrea program

Reading program for ages 6 to 8 · under construction

Section under construction (November 12, 2026). Leetrea is completed and in the testing phase. This section brings together what can already be said from the platform’s activity catalog and the full database; the program team will complete the description of the program. Translated from the Spanish version of November 27, 2026.

What it is

Leetrea is the LEEDUCA group’s program for working on reading at ages 6 to 8 (Grades 1 to 3). It follows on from Preleo, which works on the precursors in preschool, and, like Preleo, it is hosted on the LEEDUCA platform, alongside the assessment tasks.

  • Screening. The platform’s tasks for ages 6 to 11 are part of the Leetrea screening. The reading, speed and comprehension task sheets in this manual are therefore also the technical basis of the program.
  • Tiers. On the platform, Leetrea has a universal part (for the whole class) and a review part (for the children who need it), like Preleo.

  • Alphabetic code: letters and sounds, syllables and words, with activities such as “Letters that speak”, “Build the syllable”, “I put words together” or “Alien dictation”.
  • Word recognition and morphology: word reading and a “Morfoteca” (morphology library) to identify, classify, match, build and explain morphemes.
  • Fluency and prosody: from basic phrases to growing phrases, punctuation marks, theater, and poems and songs.
  • Comprehension and vocabulary: rephrasing, anaphora, local, elaborative and integrative inferences, macrorules, main ideas, outlines and vocabulary.
