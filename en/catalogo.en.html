Task catalog

Status: first version (October 21, 2026), generated automatically from the master dictionary, the platform’s activity catalog and the reliability analyses. English names and constructs have been reviewed by hand; the paragraphs for publications are an automatic draft: before using them in an article, check the figures against the task sheet or the data for the session being analyzed. English edition of November 27, 2026. Instructions and constructs not yet translated are shown in Spanish and marked (ES).

This catalog brings together in one place the 126 platform tasks of the 2010–2015 cohorts (110 assessment tasks; the rest are experimental or under review). For each task it states what it measures, how it is administered, the codes it has in the data, the sessions and ages in which it was given, the measures it records and its psychometric properties. Each one includes a paragraph ready for the instruments section of an article, in Spanish and in English.

How to use it. Search by name, code or construct, or filter by domain or by task family (the measurement model the tasks share; in Spanish). Click on a task to see its summary sheet. The “Copy” buttons copy the paragraph to the clipboard. The full table can be downloaded: Catalogo.csv (one row per task) and Sesiones.csv (one row per task, cohort and session).

What each field means.

  • Time point = age.term: 5.1 is the first term of the final year of preschool (age 5); 7.1, the first term of Grade 2.
  • Codes: the task’s base code on the platform. The same task may have several codes (versions or forms).
  • α (2021): internal consistency computed in 2021 from item-level responses. In time-limited tasks it overestimates reliability.
  • Stability: rank correlation (ρ) between two successive administrations to the same children (median over session pairs 12 months apart or less). It mixes measurement error and real change, so it is a lower bound of reliability.

The LEEDUCA platform task “Syllable counting” (code 523; LEEDUCA battery) assesses syllable awareness (segmentation). Each item is presented with a picture on the screen; the examiner gives the instruction orally and records the child’s response. It comprises 20 items. The time limit is 120 seconds. It is administered individually on a computer at school. In the 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014 and 2015 cohorts it was administered in 9 sessions, from 4 to 5 years of age. The main outcome is the number of correct responses. In our data (2021 analyses), internal consistency was α = .86 (N = 2,202, 20 items). Stability across successive administrations (median interval of 12 months) was ρ = .34 (median of 3 session pairs; range .32–.40).

Cada ítem se presenta con una imagen en la pantalla; el evaluador da la consigna oralmente y registra la respuesta del niño. Consta de 20 ítems. El tiempo máximo es de 120 segundos. Se aplica de forma individual, con ordenador, en el propio centro escolar. En las cohortes 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014 y 2015 se aplicó en 9 sesiones, de 4 a 5 años. La medida principal es el número de aciertos. En nuestros datos (análisis de 2021), la consistencia interna fue α = .86 (N = 2.202, 20 ítems). La estabilidad entre aplicaciones sucesivas (separadas por una mediana de 12 meses) fue ρ = .34 (mediana de 3 pares de sesiones; rango .32–.40).

How it is generated

The catalog is built with scripts/R/catalogo_tareas.R from four sources:

  1. The master dictionary of the 2010–2015 cohorts: tasks, sessions, measures and N.
  2. A hand-reviewed, editable table of Spanish and English names and constructs: catalogo_tareas_meta_v1.csv.
  3. The platform’s activity catalog: battery, format and instructions.
  4. The 2021 internal consistency analyses.

Stability is recomputed from the long table of scores. No items or answer keys are published: only a general description of the instructions.

Pending: α and ω for the new cohorts from item-level responses; validity against persistent reading difficulty for each task; review of the tasks flagged “under review” or with a low-stability warning; English translation of the remaining instructions and constructs.
